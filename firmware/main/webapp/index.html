This box

Before you give the box to someone else, or to start over: the box forgets everything and restarts like new. That is every person, friend box, group and message, its name, radio settings, network lock, Bluetooth PIN, the internet Wi-Fi and its password, and the Meshtastic and MeshCore settings. This phone forgets its chats here too.

Boxes in range

Bars show how well your box hears theirs. Gray = not heard for 45 s.

Network lock

–
Locked boxes encrypt every message, and ignore boxes with another passphrase. The check code must be the same on every box. The box never shows the passphrase again.

Radio

All boxes must use the same frequency, spreading factor and bandwidth, or they can't hear each other.

Box mode

Switching restarts the box. In Meshtastic or MeshCore mode, use their app over Bluetooth. To come back to FreeMesh, hold the mode button (D1) for 5 s: the light pulses, then the box restarts.

Update

–
The box joins that Wi-Fi, downloads the newest FreeMesh, Meshtastic and MeshCore, and restarts by itself. It takes a few minutes. Keep the box powered.

Using your phone's hotspot? Type its name and password, tap the button, then turn the hotspot on. The box looks for it for 10 minutes. When the box's Wi-Fi is back, join it again to see the result here.

Public Wi-Fi with a sign-in page does not work.
